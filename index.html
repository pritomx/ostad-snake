This Privacy Policy describes how the Snake Game app published by Automata Studios (the "App", "we", "us") handles information. Information We Collect

The App itself does not require account creation and does not collect personally identifiable information (name, email, phone number) directly. The following data may be processed:

  • Gameplay data — level progress and high scores, stored locally on your device only (via shared_preferences). This data is never transmitted off the device by the App itself.
  • Advertising data — if the build you're using includes ads, Google AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), IP address, coarse location, and app usage data to serve and measure ads. See "Third-Party Advertising" below.

Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

This App uses Google AdMob to display the following ad formats:

  • Banner — a small ad strip at the bottom of the Level Select and Game screens.
  • Native — ads styled to match the app, shown as a tile every 10 levels in the Level Select grid.
  • Interstitial — a full-screen ad shown after some Game Over screens.
  • Rewarded — an optional full-screen ad, shown only if you choose to "Watch ad to revive" after Game Over.

AdMob may collect and process data as described in Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use AdMob

Data AdMob may process includes advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, and interactions with ads, used to serve ads, measure performance, and (where permitted) personalize ads.

Children's Privacy

This App is a general-audience demo and is not specifically directed at children. If you publish a version of this app that may be accessed by children, you are responsible for configuring AdMob's child-directed treatment / ad content settings appropriately and complying with applicable laws (e.g. COPPA, GDPR-K).

Data Retention

Locally stored gameplay data (level progress, high scores) remains on your device until the App is uninstalled or its storage is cleared. We do not operate a backend server and do not retain this data ourselves.

Your Choices

  • You can reset high scores at any time from the in-app Settings screen.
  • You can limit ad personalization through your device's ad settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking).
  • You can uninstall the App at any time to remove all locally stored data.
